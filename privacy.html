Twinmote privacy policy

Updated November 10, 2026.

Twinmote has no sign-up, ads, tracking or usage analytics. The only data handled off the device is the app's purchase history, used to unlock Twinmote lifetime. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

The TVs you save (name, local network address and the pairing keys the TV itself provides), the free-trial start date and the purchase status are stored only on your device.

To find and control your TVs, the app talks directly to them over your Wi-Fi network or through the phone's infrared emitter; nothing goes through our servers.

The free trial and the purchase are processed by Google Play or the App Store under Google's and Apple's privacy policies. We never receive your payment details.

To confirm the purchase and unlock it on all your devices, the app uses RevenueCat (privacy policy), which receives the store receipt (product, date and country) tied to an anonymous identifier created by the app, not linked to your name, email or phone. To request deletion of this history, use the contact below.
